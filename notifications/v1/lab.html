What is live in this Lab?

In-app notifications and policy routing are working locally in this browser. External delivery methods are simulated until a trusted backend and provider credentials are configured.

In-appWorking in LabStored in this browser for testing.

Restore demo defaults clears only the Notifications Lab settings saved in this browser and reloads the original demo. It does not alter any production app or shared-library code.
