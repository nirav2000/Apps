Coverage

Apps report only after they load https://nirav2000.github.io/Apps/app-monitor.js. The tracker sends an early snapshot, then about every five minutes while used, plus a best-effort update when hidden or closed. It uses Cloudflare R2 and creates no Firebase reads or writes.
