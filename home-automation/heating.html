Why no gas cost? Wiser reports room demand, but several zones can call together. Boiler modulation, hot-water demand and cycling mean that adding room hours cannot give gas kWh. A gas meter/boiler energy sensor is needed.
Need a more accurate Warmup figure? Install an appropriate energy monitor on the electric UFH circuit, or use the manufacturer's reported energy value if available. Wattage × runtime is only an estimate.
