How the cloud monitor works

Counters are recorded locally as Firestore calls happen. Active sessions upload a cumulative R2 snapshot after about 30 seconds and then at most every five minutes, with a best-effort sync when the page is hidden or closed. The monitor itself creates no Firestore reads or writes. Google Cloud Monitoring remains the authoritative project-wide total.
